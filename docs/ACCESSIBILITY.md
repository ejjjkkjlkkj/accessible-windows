# Native accessibility

Accessibility is intrinsic to Qevarynox. It is not a framework, API, plugin, service or optional subsystem.

Human meaning exists before presentation. Visual output is one projection beside speech, braille and haptics.

Interactive artifacts require semantic identity, complete keyboard operation, a native non-visual path, structured errors/status/progress, and accessible recovery.

Non-interactive artifacts are not exempt: they must preserve semantic/provenance records and cannot introduce a visual-only diagnostic or emergency bypass.

Early boot, recovery, authentication, security, development tools, cognition, updates and maintenance have the same obligation.

The canonical invariant is `QEVARYNOX.ACCESS.FOUNDATION.NO-HUMAN-PATH-WITHOUT-SEMANTICS`.

Evidence A is structural. Evidence B requires independent behavior and cannot be satisfied by file presence, hashing or checkout equality.
